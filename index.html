About Sharodia
আসছে বছর আবার হবে।

sharodia is a small, hand-built listening room for the season we wait for all year: Mahalaya dawn, Puja nights, dhaak on the wind, and the feeling of being home in Bangladesh.

All tracks here use clearly labeled audio songs samples. They are not the Bengali recordings named in the playlist.
